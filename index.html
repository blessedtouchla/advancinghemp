About Victoria

Hemp building is my home industry.

I’ve been in hemp since 2015, when I started my first hemp company. By 2019 I was CEO of PowerPlants and COO of Hemp Crew. Hemp can do so much, but building is the part I always come back to.

I’m, in my own words, “completely crazy over hempcrete, as well as all things hemp in development and renovation!” Back in 2020 I was already collecting pictures of hemp buildings from around the world, and I called them “examples of my dreams come to life. real life developments using hemp.”

In 2022 I started the Advancing Hemp blog to share what I was learning about codes, builders and materials. This site picks up where that blog left off, with more up-to-date sources and a daily news feed.

Passionate about hemp, passionate about building. Building with hemp is a dream come true.

The mission

Legal hemp building in Los Angeles

My goal is to bring hemp building law to the city of Los Angeles, so we can legally build with hemp in cities. I want to help write “a sort of hemp-code guideline so that more people can get approved.”

Hands in the mix

Learning by building

In spring 2026 I went to a hands-on hempcrete workshop in Hawaii, on the Tiny Hemp Hale build. I also keep a box of hemp building samples (hemp rebar, hemp fiber board, a small hempcrete kit) so I can feel what I’m writing about.

The dream

A hemp home in Desert Hot Springs

I want to build a hemp home on my lot in Desert Hot Springs. For now that’s a goal, not an approved plan. When it moves forward, I’ll share every step here, including the permits.
