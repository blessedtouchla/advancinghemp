My short summaries of posts I shared on Facebook, credited to the original creators. The original graphics stay with their creators, so follow the links to see them.

Aug 9 · Hemp Building Australia

“Insulation slows heat transfer, it doesn’t create heat”

A plain-language reminder that insulation doesn’t heat or cool a room. It slows heat moving through the wall. The graphic shows how a hemp-lime wall behaves in hot weather and in cold weather.

Graphic shared in the Hemp Building Australia group. Open the group →

Aug 10 & Aug 27 · Hemployment Australia

“If Australia built one hemp factory tomorrow…”

A what-if about one hemp processing factory: the building products it could make and the jobs it could create. It’s the supply-chain story every country is working through.

Original graphic by Hemployment Australia. Find Hemployment Australia →

Jul 10 · Tiny Hemp Homes × Hempitecture

HempWool batts headed to Santa Barbara

Tiny Hemp Homes shared that they were ordering Hempitecture’s 7.5″ HempWool batts for a Santa Barbara project. It’s nice to see hemp insulation going into California homes.

Original post by Tiny Hemp Homes. About HempWool →

Jun 13 · Hemp building costs

“The cost of building with hemp: understanding the trade-offs”

An honest look at why hemp can cost more today (upfront cost, processing, transport, training, a young market) and the long-term benefits on the other side of the ledger.

Shared graphic; original creator not recorded in my notes. My take on costs →
